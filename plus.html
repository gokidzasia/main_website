Why MLBB?

A Bridge To Young People

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a familiar space for many Filipino youth. GOKidz Asia Plus uses that space wisely: not to celebrate unhealthy gaming habits, but to build relationships, teach values, and guide young people toward responsible technology use.

Vision

To build a safe and dynamic space where young people grow through disciplined gaming, free from addiction, and shaped by life-giving reflections.

Mission

To lead young gamers in a safe space where they can connect, compete, and grow, transforming gaming into a tool for discipline, life lessons, and discipleship.

Objectives

What Players Practice

Responsible Gaming

Guiding players toward self-control, balanced habits, and wise use of technology.

Sportsmanship

Building friendship, respect, teamwork, and healthy competition in a safe environment.

Small Groups

Using guided discussions and biblical principles to help participants reflect on faith and purpose.

Creative Skills

Equipping youth in video editing, livestream support, online content, Canva design, and communication.

Program Journey

From Tournament To Discipleship

Season 2

Discipline and Teamwork

Six local teams from Sto. Angel gathered at Camp Goducate, with videos and small group discussions guiding players toward self-control and responsible gaming.

Season 3

Purpose and Identity

The program expanded to San Antonio and Sto. Angel, reminding players that their value is not only found in winning, but in discovering their God-given purpose.

Season 4

Hope and the Good News

Mobilizing Legends opened to more squads, reached about 18,000 online views, and saw former players begin leading small groups and sharing the Gospel.

Season 5

Victory Is Already Secured

The team improved livestreams, visual animation, and event production while giving discipled players assignments to serve and lead.

Season 6

Stay Connected To Jesus

The biggest expansion so far gathered 17 teams from different barangays and towns in Iloilo, reaching about 41,000 online viewers.

Moving Forward

From Players To Purpose-Driven Leaders

Mobilizing Legends is growing into a pathway for friendship, faith, leadership, digital skills, and transformation. Regular Saturday training sessions will help youth learn, serve, create, lead, and use technology in a meaningful way.
